Uttar Gujarat Kadva Patidar Samaj — Three design proposals
UTTAR GUJARAT KADVA PATIDAR SAMAJ · DESIGN REVIEW

Three complete website proposals, one set of requirements

Each design is a working site: every page, login with five roles, role-based dashboards and working create/edit/delete. Open one, log in, and click through. Use the small “Design 1 2 3” switcher in any corner to jump between them on the same page.

DESIGN 1
Heritage & Devotion

The existing reference site rebuilt and completed. Saffron and gold, devotional strip, notice ticker, committee spotlight, classic top nav and maroon admin sidebar.
DESIGN 2
Modern Platform

A premium SaaS-style product. Persistent sidebar on every page, bento home, data tables, ⌘K search and slide-over editing panels.
DESIGN 3
Bold Collective

A visual-first community brand. Floating dock navigation, giant type, swipeable rails, poster event cards, a Maa Umiya feature and a member shuffle.

Good to know

  • All three designs share the same data. An event you create in Design 2 shows up in Design 3 too.
  • Edits are saved only in this browser. Every manage screen has a “reset sample data” link.
  • Registration is a demo: it checks the form but doesn’t create an account.
  • Login is frontend-only for this demo, so it isn’t real security.
  • Member phone numbers and emails are private — only the Super Admin account can see them.
  • History is drawn from Umiya Mataji Sansthan (Unjha) and community sources; only recorded dates are shown.
  • Umiya Mataji temple photos come from Wikimedia Commons (CC licences, credited on each History page). Other photos are free stock images standing in for the Patidar Samaj’s own.
